Reading
-------
* Losses are clamped (2 ATR initial stop, no averaging down, worst trade -1.1R in 16 years) and
  winners are not (best +11.6R).  A single +3R winner pays for four full -1R losses.
* The profit is carried by a small tail: 4% of trades (>= +3R) produce 35% of all the wins,
  9% produce 56%.  That is why every early "protection" rule (break-even / trailing / take-profit
  variants) and every entry filter tested on this strategy reduced the total result.
* The per-trade edge is thin (+0.11R) - it comes from volume and patience: ~150 gold trades/year,
  a 6-loss streak happens roughly every 20 trades at a 39% win rate, and the 16-year maximum
  drawdown was 24R.  A -1R day (like 2026-10-01) is the most common single outcome there is.
* Money terms at the current settings: gold 1R ~ 0.53-0.63% in a BAD regime (half risk, ~$53-62 on
  this account, exactly today's loss) and up to 1.25% (~$125) in a good regime; the last year's
  best trade was +4.7R.
